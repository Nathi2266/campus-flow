# Personas

## Staff

Individual contributors. Focus: log time, update own tasks, submit timesheets.

## Admin

Department heads / team leads. Focus: assign tasks, review timesheets, monitor the team within their department.

## Superuser

Org operators. Focus: global dashboards, audit trail, departments and users, org-wide reporting.

## Registration default

New self-registrations are created as **staff**. Roles and department membership are assigned by seed data or superuser administration.
